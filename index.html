Payday

Move the same amount, every payday.

Weekly, fortnightly, every four weeks or monthly. Budge spreads every bill, card and saving across your paydays, so each pot gets a steady amount and nothing has to be worked out.

  • Tick each pot off as you move the money, and see what's moved against the plan.
  • Warned before it runs short. If a payment leaves a pot before its money is in, you'll know which one and when.
  • A nudge on payday. Your phone tells you what to move, and again two days before big bills and card payments.
Bills

Spot every price rise before it costs you.

Download your bank statement as a CSV and Budge checks it against your bills and cards. It runs on your device, and nothing is uploaded.

  • Price changes flagged, with one tap to update and the old price kept in its history.
  • Unknown direct debits found, so forgotten subscriptions don't slip through.
  • Deal end dates remind you a month before renewal: “up £4.00 since Nov 2025”.

Private by design

Your money stays your business.

No bank login needed
Budge never connects to your bank. You add your pay and bills, or import a statement file that's read on your device.
Locked down on the server
Each person's data is protected by the database's own security rules. Others in your household only ever see joint bills and your monthly take-home total.
Locked on your phone
Fingerprint, face or PIN to open the app. Screenshots are blocked and it's hidden in your recent apps.
Getting started

  1. Add your pay

    Your paydays and take-home, or bring in your existing budget spreadsheet.

  2. Add bills and cards

    Joint or your own, with the day each one goes out and which pot pays it.

  3. Move what it says

    On payday, move the amounts into your pots and tick them off. That's it until next payday.
